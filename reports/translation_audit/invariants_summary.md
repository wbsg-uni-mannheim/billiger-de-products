Classes: codes = identifier-like tokens mixing letters and digits or four-plus digit numbers (model and
article numbers), measures = numbers with a unit (units mapped to English equivalents), numbers = every
number. Every miss is listed in `invariant_misses.csv` for inspection. The remaining misses are
dominated by (a) legitimate normalisations by the translation (37EU -> EU 37, LK5/112 -> PCD5/112,
37 1/2 -> 37.5), (b) numbers split by a missing decimal point in the German source (1 7 l -> 1.7 L),
and (c) encoding artefacts in the German source (literal unicode escapes such as u00FC, mojibake
such as GemÃ1⁄4se) that the translation resolved.

Source artefacts found on the way: 886 German offers contain literal unicode escape sequences,
51 contain mojibake. The English versions contain almost none, as the translation model
resolved them.
